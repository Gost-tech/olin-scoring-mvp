# Mexico official-source starting points

Checked 27 August 2026. Verify current text and reform dates before every substantive review.

## Data and evidence

- [LFPDPPP, current text](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPDPPP.pdf) — private-sector personal data, notices, consent, ARCO rights, responsible/processor arrangements, security, and transfers. The current compilation reviewed showed a latest reform date of 14 November 2025.
- [Regulations under the LFPDPPP](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/regla.htm) — confirm how the 2011 regulation operates with the 2025 replacement law.
- [LRSIC, current text](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LRSIC.pdf) — credit information societies, users, credit reports, authorization, and handling restrictions. The compilation reviewed showed a latest reform date of 24 January 2024.
- [Código de Comercio, current text](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/CCom.pdf) — messages of data, attribution, electronic signatures, and commercial evidence. Confirm the latest 2026 amendments before relying on an article number.
- [NOM-151-SCFI-2016](https://dof.gob.mx/normasOficiales.php?codp=6499&view=si) — preservation of data messages and digitization. Applicability and implementation require counsel and technical-provider confirmation.

## Financial perimeter and conduct

- [Ley para Regular las Instituciones de Tecnología Financiera](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LRITF.pdf) — ITF activities, authorizations, models, and API framework. The compilation reviewed showed a latest reform date of 14 November 2025.
- [Ley de Protección y Defensa al Usuario de Servicios Financieros](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LPDUSF.pdf) — user protection and CONDUSEF perimeter. The compilation reviewed showed a latest reform date of 14 November 2025.
- [CONDUSEF RECA](https://registros.condusef.gob.mx/reca/reca.php) — registry and minimum-information starting point for financial adhesion contracts. Registration does not always mean substantive approval.
- [Banco de México Circular 2/2020](https://www.banxico.org.mx/marco-normativo/normativa-emitida-por-el-banco-de-mexico/circular-2-2020/%7B4FDD6B5E-8DFA-F095-6325-68C388AAEAA0%7D.pdf) — standardized APIs under article 76 of the Fintech Law. Do not infer that Olin qualifies for access.
- [LFPIORPI, current text](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFPIORPI.pdf) — vulnerable activities and AML obligations. The compilation reviewed showed a latest reform date of 16 July 2025.

## Research discipline

- Quote no article number from memory.
- Record the official URL, access date, effective/reform date shown, exact article, and factual assumption to which it is applied.
- Distinguish a binding rule from regulator guidance, registry information, provider contract requirements, and market practice.
- If Olin remains only a B2B shadow decision-support vendor, ask counsel which financial rules apply indirectly through the bank contract and which apply directly to Olin.
