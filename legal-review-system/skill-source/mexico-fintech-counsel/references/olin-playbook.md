# Olin shadow-pilot legal playbook

## Approved factual posture

- Olin is an explainable, rules-based decision-support prototype.
- The first proposed engagement is a bank-controlled shadow pilot of no more than ten authorized cases.
- The institution keeps its official credit policy, analysis, decision, customer relationship, and regulatory responsibilities.
- Olin does not offer, approve, contract, fund, disburse, service, collect, or move money in the shadow pilot.
- Synthetic testing proves software controls, not repayment prediction or legal compliance.
- Production data connectors, Círculo authority, real outcomes, and institutional approvals remain to be proven.

## Required contract positions for the shadow pilot

- Expressly exclude credit offers, automated final decisions, customer contracting, disbursement, servicing, collections, and payment initiation.
- Set a ten-case ceiling and define whether cases are historical, current, anonymized, pseudonymized, or identifiable.
- Require the bank to document its authority to supply each data element and its independent decision.
- Identify the personal-data `responsable` and `encargado` roles only after counsel confirms them; include processing instructions and an approved data exhibit.
- Define allowed sources, prohibited raw credentials, evidence references, purpose limitation, and no secondary model training unless separately agreed and lawfully supported.
- Define retention, deletion/return, correction, consent withdrawal, incident, audit, subprocessor, hosting, and cross-border rules.
- Prohibit publicity, logo use, and claims of partnership without written permission.
- Make launch conditional on bank security, privacy, legal/compliance, credit/model-risk, engineering, and UAT approvals.
- Measure workflow time, coverage, missing evidence, agreement, override reasons, incidents, and audit completeness. Do not make a predictive-performance claim from ten cases.

## Statements requiring correction or escalation

- "Olin complies with Mexican law" → state the exact reviewed control and keep legal compliance subject to counsel/partner review.
- "Secure" or "encrypted" → specify what is encrypted, where, under which configuration, and what remains plaintext.
- "Consent obtained" → specify the text version, purpose, identity, channel, timestamp, evidence, withdrawal state, and who is authorized to rely on it.
- "Production-ready" → say which technical gate passed and list external gates still open.
- "Partner" → use "prospective partner" or name the institution only with written permission.
- "Approval" → distinguish Olin recommendation from the institution's decision.
- "Validated" → name the validation type, dataset, owner, date, and limitations.

## Lawyer decisions to obtain

1. Olin's contracting entity and exact regulatory perimeter for the facts of the shadow pilot.
2. Responsible/processor allocation and whether any party is an independent responsible party.
3. Permitted data categories, sources, transfers/remissions, hosting locations, subprocessors, and retention.
4. Merchant notice and consent artifacts, including any separate Círculo authorization.
5. Whether pseudonymized or historical files can reduce scope and under what conditions.
6. Required contract form, signatures, evidence preservation, dispute forum, tax treatment, and insurance.
7. What must change before a funded or live-decision phase.
