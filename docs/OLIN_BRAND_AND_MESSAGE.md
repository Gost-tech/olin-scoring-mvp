# Olin brand and message system

## The decision

Olin should look like serious credit infrastructure with the warmth of the businesses it serves. It should not look like a consumer loan app, a crypto product, or a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Visual thesis

**Institutional product theatre with Mexican warmth:** a mineral-dark decision canvas, warm paper, and one electric signal travelling through a transparent system.

## Content plan

1. **Hero:** What Olin does, for whom, and its honest maturity.
2. **Proof:** What is built and tested in the current prototype.
3. **Mechanism:** Evidence to tier to human decision to outcome.
4. **Pilot:** A controlled 10–30 case shadow pilot with explicit roles.
5. **CTA:** Design the pilot or open the analyst demo.

## Interaction thesis

- The Olin trace draws once on entry and then becomes still.
- A sample dossier visibly moves through Círculo, DSCR, and internal-score gates into committee review.
- A vertical progress rail connects evidence, decision, and outcome while scrolling.

Every motion is finite, fast, and optional. Reduced-motion users receive the same information without animation.

## Core message

### Spanish

**Convierte la operación cotidiana de un abarrotes en una decisión de crédito auditable.**

Olin combina Círculo, capacidad de pago y evidencia operativa en una recomendación explicable. El originador conserva la decisión.

### English

**Turn a neighborhood merchant's daily operations into an auditable credit recommendation.**

Olin combines bureau, affordability, and verified operating evidence in an explainable recommendation. The lender keeps the decision.

### French founder version

**Olin transforme les preuves d'activité quotidiennes d'un commerce de quartier en recommandation de crédit explicable.**

Olin ne remplace pas le prêteur. Il organise les preuves, applique une politique transparente et aide le comité à prendre une décision traçable.

## The honest maturity line

`Prototipo funcional · listo para piloto sombra · no originación automática`

This line should be visible near the first CTA and repeated near the final disclaimer.

## Logo concept

The symbol is an open `o` made from one traced path and one moving point. The opening represents a decision that can be inspected, not a closed black box. The point represents an operating signal entering the system.

Rules:

- The symbol must work in one color at 16 pixels.
- The wordmark is always lowercase: `olin`.
- Do not add coins, cards, shields, arrows, charts, or bank-building imagery.
- Do not imitate Indigenous iconography. The possible Nahuatl association of *ollin* should not become a public heritage claim without cultural and legal review.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Obsidian | `#0B1916` | Primary dark canvas |
| Mineral | `#12362F` | Panels and secondary dark surfaces |
| Canvas | `#F4F0E7` | Warm light background |
| Signal | `#C8FA5A` | One action, live path, positive state |
| Terracotta | `#C55232` | Caution or decline only |
| Muted ink | `#60716B` | Supporting text on light surfaces |

Acid lime is the only brand accent. Terracotta is semantic, not decorative.

## Typography

- Display: a restrained editorial serif or high-contrast display face for short headlines.
- Body and product: a contemporary grotesk with excellent numerals.
- Maximum two type families.
- Use tabular numerals for scores, ratios, tiers, and timestamps.

The static website uses resilient system fallbacks so that it still works offline.

## Voice

- Precise, not grandiose.
- Ambitious, not speculative.
- Human, not sentimental.
- Technical only when the detail proves control.

### Say this

- `recomendación explicable`
- `evidencia verificada`
- `piloto sombra`
- `el originador conserva la decisión`
- `prototipo funcional`
- `36 combinaciones probadas`

### Do not say this yet

- `IA que predice el default`
- `89% de precisión`
- `crédito en 38 minutos`
- `alianza con Monex/FEMSA/Bimbo`
- `95% olvidado por los bancos`
- `producción lista`
- `retorno 10–15x`

## Presentation principle

Show one real mechanism per screen. The product should be the proof: source state, Círculo band, DSCR, internal score, tier, reasons, analyst action, and final outcome. Decorative charts never replace evidence.
