# Olin promotion strategy — controlled-pilot demand generation

## Commercial objective

Generate qualified conversations with Mexican credit institutions for a controlled, parallel pilot of 10 authorized cases. The campaign should sell a low-risk learning process, not make claims about predictive performance that Olin has not yet validated.

## Positioning

**Audience:** Heads of Credit, Risk, Product, Data, and Operations at banks, SOFOMes, fintech lenders, acquirers, and merchant networks serving Mexican small businesses.

**Problem:** Evidence about a small business is fragmented across credit history, cash-flow signals, operating context, permissions, and missing data. That makes a recommendation hard to review and defend.

**Promise:** Olin turns authorized evidence into a reviewable credit recommendation with visible sources, gaps, rules, and reasons. The lender keeps the final decision.

**Commercial offer:** A shadow pilot using 10 authorized cases, run in parallel to the institution's existing process, with no money movement.

**Primary message:** “10 casos reales. Sin mover dinero. Un proceso de decisión que su equipo puede defender.”

**CTA:** “Revisemos un expediente, no una promesa.”

## Campaign architecture

### 1. Attention — 15-second vertical teaser

Hook with “10 casos reales. Sin mover dinero.” Show only three proof moments: evidence, explained recommendation, lender-owned decision. CTA sends viewers to the pilot page or a direct conversation.

### 2. Consideration — 37-second square LinkedIn film

Use the `olin-ten-cases` campaign video. Publish natively from a founder or operator account, then repost from the company page. The post copy should explain why the pilot is deliberately bounded and invite credit leaders to challenge one case together.

### 3. Evaluation — 60–90-second product walkthrough

Use the existing end-to-end demo only after a prospect engages. Tailor the walkthrough to the prospect's policy and data reality; do not lead with a generic feature tour.

### 4. Conversion — pilot memo and working session

Offer a 30-minute case review, then a short pilot memo covering authorized inputs, inclusion criteria, review roles, measures, stop/go criteria, and data handling. The outcome is a pilot decision, not a software purchase commitment.

## LinkedIn launch sequence

### Post 1 — Risk reversal

**Opening:** “Antes de integrar otra herramienta al proceso de crédito, pruebe diez casos. En paralelo. Sin mover dinero.”

Explain the bounded pilot and end with: “Si lidera Crédito o Riesgo en una institución mexicana, revisemos un expediente juntos.”

### Post 2 — Explainability

Show the evidence and recommendation frames. Explain that missing information remains visible and that the recommendation includes the reasons and route—not just a score.

### Post 3 — Human authority

Show analyst review and outcome recording. State clearly that Olin does not make the institution's final decision.

### Post 4 — What the pilot measures

Discuss coverage, handling time, disagreements, missing evidence, and usefulness. Do not publish invented benchmarks; use the post to define what would make a pilot worth continuing.

## Distribution and outreach

- Publish the main film natively on LinkedIn with Spanish captions and a strong first-frame hook.
- Send the film in targeted founder-led outreach only after a personalized opening tied to the institution's segment.
- Ask warm contacts for introductions to the person who owns credit policy or portfolio risk, not a generic innovation inbox.
- Use the pilot memo and product walkthrough as follow-up assets; avoid attaching every asset in the first message.
- Build one landing-page route around the pilot offer, with a calendar or direct email CTA and clear qualification questions.

## Qualification

A strong pilot prospect has an identifiable credit-policy owner, access to 10 authorized historical or current cases, willingness to compare Olin with the existing process, and agreement on what can be measured. Deprioritize prospects seeking guaranteed approvals, instant production integration, or performance claims before validation.

## Measurement

Track the funnel by institution and role:

- Qualified target accounts reached
- Video views past 3 seconds and 50%
- Replies from Credit/Risk decision owners
- Case-review meetings booked
- Pilot memos sent
- Controlled pilots approved
- Cases completed with usable evidence
- Pilot stop/go decisions and documented reasons

Optimize for qualified conversations and pilot decisions, not raw impressions.

## Claim guardrails

- Say “recommendation,” not “approval.”
- Say “estimates capacity” and “organizes risk evidence,” not “knows who will pay.”
- Say “prototipo funcional” or describe the exact implemented capability; do not imply production readiness.
- Label synthetic cases and examples.
- Do not claim accuracy, ROI, partnerships, live integrations, or portfolio outcomes until evidence exists.
- Repeat that the institution owns the final decision.
