# Elektra meeting card

## Say this first

> **“Olin convierte un ‘no por falta de papeles’ en una ruta verificable: qué evidencia alternativa ya existe, qué falta, cuánto pago puede soportar el negocio y por qué. Banco Azteca conserva la decisión.”**

## The problem we solve

Existing or prospective productive-credit customers can have real business activity but incomplete conventional income documentation. The current process may require repeated documents, a visit or manual reconstruction across disconnected data.

## What Olin does

1. Reuses Banco Azteca identity, consent, bureau and relationship results.
2. Selects the appropriate evidence route: bank flow, TPV, fiscal, supplier, receivables or cash triangulation.
3. Verifies source, subject, freshness, consent and provenance.
4. Adds business permanence, location and sector context only where relevant.
5. Calculates stressed repayment capacity under Banco Azteca terms.
6. Returns: evidence-supported review, missing alternative, safer amount or not-yet-supportable.
7. Banco Azteca makes and records the official decision.

## Important product choice

Do not use psychometric, WhatsApp response-time or device-style signals in the first credit pilot. Use transactional, supplier, settlement, permanence and capacity evidence first.

## Ask for one pilot

> “Seleccionemos casos de Mi Negocio que quedaron pendientes o en revisión por evidencia insuficiente. Reproducimos el proceso con información histórica permitida, medimos cuántos casos obtienen una ruta verificable y cuánto tiempo ahorra el analista. No cambiamos decisiones hasta que Riesgos valide.”

## Ask these questions

1. What missing-document reason has the highest volume?
2. At what step do customers abandon or enter manual review?
3. Which bank, TPV, supplier, visit or repayment datasets already exist but are disconnected?
4. What is the baseline analyst time and mature delinquency outcome?
5. Which exact customer journey should be the pilot?

## Do not promise

- Approval for everybody.
- A validated default model.
- Production readiness.
- That Google, WhatsApp or psychometrics prove repayment.
- That collateral replaces capacity.

## Desired meeting outcome

- One named journey.
- One credit-policy owner.
- One data/integration owner.
- Historical-data dictionary and outcome definition.
- A technical validation workshop date.
