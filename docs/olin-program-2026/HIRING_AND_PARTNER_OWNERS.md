## Olin hiring order

### 1. Fractional Mexico fintech counsel and privacy lead — contract first

**Outcome:** approve the pilot agreement, data roles, privacy notice, Círculo
authorization, e-signature evidence, retention, complaints boundary, and the
future lender/originator structure.

**Evidence to request from candidates:** Mexican financial-sector matters,
SOFOM/bank vendor contracts, LRSIC, privacy, electronic contracting/NOM-151,
and references from a lender or regulated fintech.

**Post:**

> Olin seeks fractional Mexican fintech counsel for a bank-controlled SME
> credit-decisioning pilot. You will turn existing drafts into approved pilot,
> data-processing, outcome-data, consent, retention, and vendor-access terms.
> The current product is shadow decision support and does not lend or move
> money. Please send two relevant anonymized matters, your availability for a
> two-week sprint, conflict disclosures, scope assumptions, and a fixed-fee
> range. Mexican financial-services privacy, LRSIC, e-signature/NOM-151, and
> bank/SOFOM contracting experience are required.

### 2. Founding Credit & Risk Lead — first core hire

**Outcome:** co-design bank policy, own evidence-route definitions, run UAT,
review all ten cases, and define the larger validation cohort.

**Must have:** at least five years in Mexican SME/micro-business underwriting;
real policy or portfolio authority; ability to explain cash-flow and thin-file
decisions; experience with overrides, collections feedback, and model risk.

**Post:**

> Olin is building an evidence-first decision workspace for Mexican small
> businesses that do not fit document-heavy credit processes. We need a
> founding Credit & Risk Lead to define transparent evidence routes with a
> lender partner—bank flow, TPV, supplier, fiscal and cash-observed—not to train
> a black-box model from ten cases. You will own policy workshops, exception
> rules, UAT, case review, outcome definitions and the validation roadmap.
> Success in the first 60 days is a signed policy, ten safely completed shadow
> cases, zero hidden missingness, and an agreed outcome feed. Send one policy
> you helped change, one adverse decision you defended, and how you would treat
> a cash-heavy merchant with no formal statements.

### 3. Founding Backend/Security Engineer — second core hire

**Outcome:** finish the PostgreSQL runtime, approved-cloud deployment, SSO/MFA,
tenant isolation, observability, backup/restore, and provider callbacks.

**Must have:** Python/PostgreSQL, API security, cloud/IaC, OAuth/OIDC, audit and
incident response. Bank/fintech production experience preferred.

**Post:**

> Olin needs a pragmatic founding backend/security engineer to take an audited
> Python shadow-pilot system into a bank-approved production environment. The
> job is PostgreSQL, migrations, OIDC/MFA, tenant claims, immutable events,
> provider webhooks, secrets, monitoring, backups and restore—not microservices.
> You will ship controls that a bank can test and explain them to security and
> procurement. The first work sample is a two-hour design for migrating a
> fail-closed SQLite pilot to managed PostgreSQL without exposing real cases.
> Please include one incident you handled and one access-control boundary you
> implemented.

### 4. Pilot Operations & Risk Analyst — hire when pilot dates are signed

**Outcome:** own daily exceptions, evidence corrections, consent withdrawal,
provider outages, alert acknowledgment, SLA timing, UAT evidence, and the kill
switch escalation.

**Post:**

> Olin seeks a Pilot Operations & Risk Analyst for a ten-case controlled bank
> pilot. You will not approve or fund loans. You will make every case complete,
> traceable and measurable: verify evidence status, route exceptions, record
> analyst time, manage corrections/withdrawals, acknowledge provider alerts and
> maintain the daily control log. Success is 100% consent/provenance/audit
> coverage and no unresolved critical exception. Credit operations, KYC,
> underwriting support or fintech back-office experience in Mexico is required.

## Roles not to hire yet

- Large sales team: founder should sell until one channel repeats.
- ML/data-science team: ten cases cannot support model development.
- Dedicated DevOps/SRE team: one strong backend/security generalist plus managed
  services is sufficient for the pilot.
- Collections team: the lending partner owns collections in the shadow and first
  live phases unless contracts and regulation intentionally change that role.

## Interview scorecard

Score 1–5 on evidence, not charisma:

- direct Mexico SME credit or bank-production experience;
- judgment under incomplete evidence;
- written clarity and audit discipline;
- ability to state what must remain human-controlled;
- concrete first-30-day deliverable;
- reference quality and conflicts;
- comfort working hands-on in a four-person company.

Reject anyone who promises automatic approvals from public web data, treats
collateral as repayment capacity, proposes ML before outcomes, or cannot explain
how they stop a process safely.
