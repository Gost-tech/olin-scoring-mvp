# Deterministic skill analysis

## Pricing model

The pricing-model tool ranked usage-based at 75/100 and hybrid at 70/100 from
the Olin brief. The human recommendation remains **hybrid**—platform fee plus
included volume and transparent overage—because a bank needs budget
predictability and Olin has no current deal or willingness-to-pay sample. This
is a conscious tradeoff, not a claim that the tool chose the final price.

No Van Westendorp result is reported: Olin has fewer than the required 30
qualified willingness-to-pay responses. The price ranges in the canonical
report are test ranges.

## Pilot process

The process-mapping tools analyzed an explicitly estimated, not measured,
eight-stage shadow case:

- P50 total: 2,305 minutes;
- P90 total: 7,785 minutes;
- value-add ratio: 3.7%;
- wait ratio: 83.3%;
- rework ratio: 13.0%;
- verdict: waste-heavy.

The estimated constraints are waiting for applicant evidence/consent (P50 one
day), waiting for the bank analyst queue (P50 eight hours), and evidence rework
(P50 four hours). The product response is Evidence Passport guidance,
route-specific completeness, a WIP limit of three pilot cases, and a single
operational queue. Replace every estimate with measured timestamps during the
pilot before making staffing or SLA decisions.
