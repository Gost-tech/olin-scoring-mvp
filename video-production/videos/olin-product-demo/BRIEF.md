# Olin product demo V3

- Audience: fintech angels, lender executives, SOFOM and bank product/risk teams
- Goal: prove that Olin is a working decision-workflow product, not a landing page
- Runtime: 66 seconds
- Languages: Spanish and English
- Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps target
- Voice: existing HeyGen female voices (Camila Vega ES, Blanka EN)
- Truth boundary: synthetic data, no customer claim, no live bureau/bank/POS connection, no money movement
- Product spine: case → evidence → recommendation → partner outcome
- CTA: one 10-case, four-week shadow pilot

## Non-negotiables

- Show captured states from the actual Olin demo interface at readable scale.
- Do not show a fake bank portal, simulated transfer, contract, or disbursement.
- Do not claim that Olin predicts repayment or approves loans.
- The institution keeps final decision authority.
