# Olin product demo V3 script

The demo uses six existing HeyGen narration segments in each language:

1. What Olin produces: one decision-ready case from authorized data.
2. Purpose and consent are recorded before data is checked.
3. Evidence sources, verification states and missing signals remain visible.
4. A versioned policy returns a recommendation, confidence and reasons.
5. The institution makes and records the final decision.
6. The outcome stays connected to the original case; next step is a 10-case shadow pilot.

The previous contract/disbursement narration and scene are intentionally excluded.
