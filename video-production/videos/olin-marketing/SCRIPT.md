# SCRIPT — Olin institutional launch

**Voice:** Daniel (macOS local speech)
**Voice settings:** 178 words per minute
**Voice direction:** Calm, precise, credible. No hype; sound like a product leader speaking to a credit committee.

---

## Line 1 — The unanswered question (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 8.0s
**Delivery:** Begin with scale, then slow slightly on the question.

    Mexico has millions of small businesses. But for most lenders, one question is still hard to answer: can this business repay this loan?

## Line 2 — A new evidence rail (Frame 2)

**Time:** 8.0 – 16.0s
**Delivery:** Read the signal list as distinct, measured cues.

    Olin organizes consented credit, cash-flow, POS, supplier, tenure, and operating evidence around a specific amount and term.

## Line 3 — The decision loop (Frame 3)

**Time:** 16.0 – 24.0s
**Delivery:** Firmly contrast the product with a black box.

    The result is not a black box score. It is a reviewable file: recommendation, reasons, missing evidence, and a route for human decision.

## Line 4 — Repayment is designed (Frame 4)

**Time:** 24.0 – 32.0s
**Delivery:** Emphasize that the amount can change.

    Capacity is tested against the proposed payment. If the fit is weak, Olin can route the case to review, decline it, or suggest a smaller amount.

## Line 5 — Real interface, human authority (Frame 5)

**Time:** 32.0 – 39.0s
**Delivery:** Reassuring and accountable.

    Credit teams keep final authority. Every decision and later outcome can be recorded, compared, and used to calibrate policy.

## Line 6 — Start with evidence (Frame 6)

**Time:** 39.0 – 45.0s
**Delivery:** Finish with a direct, low-risk invitation.

    Start with ten authorized cases, in shadow mode, with no money movement. Measure coverage, time, disagreement, and missing evidence before integration.
