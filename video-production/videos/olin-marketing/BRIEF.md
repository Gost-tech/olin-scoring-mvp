## Intent

Sell Olin as the credit operating layer for institutions that already own the
merchant relationship. Open with the financing gap, then show the new payment
data rail, the auditable decision loop, repayment design, and the controlled
pilot ask. The tone should feel precise, modern, credible, and institution-ready.
