## Video direction

Olin should feel like institutional product theatre with Mexican warmth: Obsidian and Mineral surfaces, Canvas breathing room, Signal lime used only for proof and action, and Terracotta used sparingly for exceptions. One controlled virtual camera; motion is smooth and decisive, never bouncy. Real product screens appear as evidence, framed like reviewable case material rather than floating mockups. Reveals follow the spoken cues across the back half of each frame. Frames 3 and 4 earn deliberate still holds so the recommendation and human authority can be read. No stock footage, no fake performance metrics, no decorative AI imagery, no infinite motion, no lazy card breathing, and no late-frame drifting camera. Keep the bottom 17% clear for Spanish captions.
